A: 0, 10, 15, 20
B: 10, 0, 35, 25
C: 15, 35, 0, 30
D: 20, 25, 30, 0

Clearly label the starting city A and the return to A. Keep all labels readable and ensure the cost values match the matrix. This is an educational algorithm diagram, not a decorative poster.
